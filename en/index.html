02 / PROCESS

From a spark
to a real thing.

Most projects do not succeed on the first try. We build a rough version that runs, then iterate. At every step, you can find tools, space and people at DIHUOLAB.

STEP 01

Idea

Talk through the problem first. The more specific the better—even if it is just “this thing keeps falling off.”

STEP 02

Test

Validate the key assumption at the lowest cost: a sketch, a piece of code, a printed part.

STEP 03

Prototype

Print, cut, wire, code. Make it actually move, even if it is still ugly.

STEP 04

Land it

Test, iterate, deliver or open-source. Turn “we tried it” into “it works,” then into “it works well.”
