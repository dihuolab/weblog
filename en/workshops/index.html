HOW IT WORKS

One manual,
three phases.

Hosts read the run of show and contingencies. Participants read the tasks and checklists. The same page works before the workshop, during it, and after it.

01

Before / Prepare

Goal, output, materials, space, equipment and safety limits. Write down anything that is still uncertain.

02

On the day / Run

Timeline, host actions, participant tasks, checkpoints and common failures.

03

After / Review

Keep what worked, record what went wrong, and write the next change back into the manual.
